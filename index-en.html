What Lukta does

  • Save from any appTap Share and pick Lukta, or paste a link or type some text.
  • Sort it your wayPriorities, tags and statuses; drag any item to move it.
  • Reminded on timeSet a reminder on anything; it arrives even offline.
  • No account neededIt works fully on your phone; an account is for reaching it from more phones.

How to install

  1. Tap “Download Lukta”, then open the file from your notifications or Downloads.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to “Install unknown apps”, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, choose “More details”, then “Install anyway”. It shows for any app from outside the store.
  4. Open Lukta and start saving.

To update later, download the new file from this page and install it over the old one. Everything you saved stays.
