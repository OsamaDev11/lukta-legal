Install and update with Google Play

  1. Tap “Get Lukta on Google Play” to open the app listing.
  2. Tap “Install” if you’re new, or “Update” if you already have an older version.
  3. Open Lukta and start saving and organizing your content.

If you originally downloaded Lukta from this website, update it through Google Play over your existing installation. Do not uninstall first, so your locally saved data remains available.
