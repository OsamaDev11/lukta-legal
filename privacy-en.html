Who we are

Lukta is an app for saving links, texts and images to come back to later, made by Wolf Brain. This page explains what the app keeps, where, why, and how you control it.

What stays on your phone

Everything you save is stored on your phone first, and the app works fully offline: links, texts and copies of the images you share with it, their titles and notes, tags, priorities and statuses, reminders, recent searches, and your appearance and language settings.

If you sign in, your session keys are kept in Android's secure storage.

If you create an account

An account is optional. With one, we need:

  • Your email and password, only to sign you in. They are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Your content, to sync it: links and texts with their titles and notes, tags, priority and status, and reminder times, so you can reach them from any device. Content is stored encrypted on the server.

Images are not uploaded. They stay on the device they were saved from.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, files or advertising ID. The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party service that collects data about you.

Permissions

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to show your reminders on time. They are scheduled on your phone.
  • Clipboard: read only when you tap Paste in the save screen.
  • Sharing from other apps: Lukta only gets what you choose to share with it.
  • Internet: to sign in and sync, when you have an account.

How we use your data

Only to run the service: keeping your content, syncing it between your devices, and signing you in. We don't sell your data, share it, or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

On your phone, and, if you have an account, on the Lukta server at a hosting provider. Data travels between the app and the server over an encrypted connection.

How long, and deleting it

  • Your account's data stays on the server for as long as the account exists.
  • When you delete your account, the account and all its data are permanently deleted from the server. We keep no copy.
  • Signing out keeps your data on your phone. It is removed from the phone when you uninstall the app, or when you choose to erase it while deleting your account.

Children

Lukta is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data about them.

Your rights

You can see, change and delete your data in the app, or write to us about any request concerning it.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects, or how it uses it, changes, we update this page and the date at its bottom.
