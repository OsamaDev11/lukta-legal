Delete your account

Two ways to delete your Lukta account and all its data.

In the app

  1. Open Settings, then tap your account card at the top.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Enter your password, then tap Delete. You can also choose to erase your data from the phone.

The account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email us from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my Lukta account". We delete the account and all its data within 30 days at most, and confirm by email.

Ask to delete my account

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email and password.
  • Everything synced: links and texts with their titles and notes, tags, and reminders.

It is permanently deleted from the server, and we keep no copy.

What is on your phone

Data saved on your phone, including images, stays there unless you choose to erase it when deleting, or uninstall the app.
